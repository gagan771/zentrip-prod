# Safety, payments, and cash in India

These are short, sourced rules Zenny must not invent beyond.

## Emergency

If a traveller is in immediate danger, tell them to call **112** now. 112 is India's single emergency number for police, fire, and ambulance. Official site: https://112.gov.in/

The Ministry of Tourism tourist helpline is **1363**.

## UPI

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is an instant, interoperable bank-to-bank payment system developed by NPCI under RBI oversight. It launched on 11 April 2016.

Eligible foreign travellers may obtain a prepaid payment instrument (PPI) wallet linked to UPI at select international airports, per RBI guidance (originally 10 February 2023 circular, G20 travellers, Bengaluru / Mumbai / New Delhi, outstanding balance cap described as Rs 2 lakh). Treat operational details as estimated and tell them to confirm current RBI/NPCI rules. Zentrip never holds money or card numbers.

## Cash and cards

Indian rupee cash is still used for small purchases, tips, and places without UPI. Use bank-branded ATMs; treat machines that ask for a PIN twice or redirect off the bank site as unsafe. International cards work at many merchants; expect issuer fees.

## What not to do

Do not invent live FX rates, do not recommend unofficial money changers as official, do not collect OTP, PIN, or card numbers.
